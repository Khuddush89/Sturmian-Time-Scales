# 🤝 Code of conduct

This project welcomes participants of every background and level of experience.
The following expectations apply to issues, pull requests, discussions, and
other spaces associated with the project.

## Expected behavior

- Discuss ideas and evidence respectfully; criticize the argument rather than the person.
- Give constructive feedback, acknowledge corrections, and credit contributions.
- Use inclusive language and respect differing experiences and accessibility needs.
- Respect privacy, consent, and reasonable limits on personal contact.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discriminatory remarks, sexualized content, personal attacks,
deliberate disruption, and publishing private information without consent are
not acceptable.

## Reporting

Report concerns privately to **khuddush89@gmail.com**. Include links and relevant
context when possible. Reports will be reviewed discreetly; information will be
shared only as needed to investigate and respond.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove content that breaches these expectations.
Responses may include a private warning, a request to stop or correct behavior,
a temporary restriction, or a permanent ban for serious or repeated violations.

Maintainers should explain decisions when it is safe to do so and consider
context, severity, and previous conduct. A participant may request reconsideration
by contacting the reporting address with additional context.
